No sales or dividends yet. Use Sell or Dividend on a position: the amount lands here and in Realized P&L.

Data: TradingView scanner, refreshed automatically (most exchanges are 15 min delayed on TradingView's free feed). Open Px = today's opening price (closed before that market opens). Purchase Px = the price per share you paid. Close Px = previous session close. Day % counts only a session that traded today: before a market opens (e.g. US stocks in the European morning) it shows closed and the pre-market move, and counts 0 in your Day figure. Double-click any column title to rank by it (again to reverse, a third time to reset). Bench % = benchmark move since your open date. Header 1 Week / 1 Month = your portfolio's own time-weighted return over that full period; if you have held it for less, it shows "–" and the return since opening underneath. Realized P&L = gains and losses from shares you sold + dividends. Unrealized P&L = what would go into your P&L if you sold every open position at today's price. The per-stock 1W/1M/YTD/1Y/10Y columns are the stock's own moves; in the total row they are your portfolio's, shown only once it is older than the period. IPO Px = first available split-adjusted close on TradingView, used for Since IPO. When a stock listed after the start of a period, those cells merge into one box showing its IPO date. Sector attribution = weight × (position return − its benchmark return), in percentage points, using TradingView sectors.
